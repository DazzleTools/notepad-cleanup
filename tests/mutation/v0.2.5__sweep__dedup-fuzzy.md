## What the survivors revealed (the payload)

The pre-existing suite executed the fuzzy pipeline but did not constrain: threshold edges (size<=1, diff==threshold, ratio==2.0, size==50KB), the "threshold of the LARGER file" promise, ALL of `parse_fuzzy_modes`' documented grammar, and -- most relevant to the provenance goal -- **both canonical-selection promises** (oldest instance wins; organized/ preferred on ties; near-match uses `_pick_canonical` at all). Those are exactly the semantics the planned `_find_near_match` optimization must preserve, which is why this sweep ran before any production change.

Bonus discovery while writing killers: `near_match_threshold(2) == 6` -- the log-quadratic inflates below its smallest anchor (10), so unrelated 2-char files can near-match. Pinned loosely (`< 10`) so any fix is deliberate; logged in the 2026-08-19 DWP decision ledger.

## Caveats (always included)

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.**
